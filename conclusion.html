Conclusion

Preserving Katy's rich history for generations to come.

Our Mission

Linking Katy's History and Today

Katy is experiencing rapid growth, and with the increasing number of non-native residents, we feel compelled to educate them about the rich history and heritage of our beloved city. Katy was renowned for its politeness, generosity, and strong sense of community, and we must strive to consistently preserve these qualities. Katy also became one of the most international cities in the states. As part of this diverse community, we are proud to share our knowledge and contribute to the preservation of Katy's roots. We hope Katy remains a trendy city, celebrated for its southern charm.

Destination Imagination Team 5 to Infinity from James E. Taylor High School

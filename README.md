# Katy — Then and Now
### Tracing Katy's journey from railroad town to modern city.

> A historical preservation website created by **Students from James E. Taylor High School - Katy, Texas**  

---

## About the Project

**Katy — Then and Now** is an interactive educational website dedicated to preserving and sharing the rich history of Katy, Texas. As one of the fastest-growing cities in the United States, Katy is home to a rapidly increasing number of residents who may be unfamiliar with its deep roots. This project was created to bridge that gap — honoring the past while celebrating the city's growth.

From its origins as a quiet railroad town known as "Cane Island" to its rise as one of Texas's most vibrant and international cities, this website documents Katy's transformation through photographs, maps, timelines, and stories passed down through generations.

**Visit our website through this link: [katythenandnow.org](https://katythenandnow.org)**

---

## Features

- **Before & After Sliders** — Drag an interactive gold slider to compare historic and modern photographs of iconic Katy locations side by side
- **Image Carousels** — Browse collections of historical and modern photographs across multiple topics
- **Interactive Maps** — Explore historical map overlays of Katy's roads, zoning, and landmarks using Leaflet.js with adjustable opacity controls
- **Timelines** — Follow the chronological story of Katy's land use and rice farming history
- **Popup Animals** — A fun hover feature designed to engage younger audiences as they explore the site
- **Fully Responsive** — Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile with a hamburger navigation menu for small screens

---

## Pages

| Page | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Introduction** | Overview of the project and what visitors will discover |
| **Photo Archives** | Before and after photo comparisons of historic Katy locations including the Katy Hotel, MKT Railroad Depot, Hotel Clardy, and more |
| **Land Use Transformations** | How Katy's land changed from rice fields and ranches to suburbs and highways, with interactive maps and timelines |
| **Culture** | Rodeo heritage at the LH7 Ranch, 1950s economy, and the legacy of AstroWorld theme park |
| **Animals** | Wildlife that once roamed Katy's prairies and how urbanization has affected local animal populations |
| **Conclusion** | Closing reflections on preserving Katy's identity and team credits |
